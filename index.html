Welcome

An avocado grove between the hills and the sea

Tucked into the gentle folds of the countryside above Tavira, Quinta do Cabeço is a family farm where rows of avocado trees give way to open sky and the scent of citrus. Here, mornings begin with birdsong and the soft green of the grove, and evenings end under a wide, unhurried sunset.

The home has been kept simple and honest — natural materials, whitewashed walls, and terracotta underfoot — a place to breathe out and let the Algarve set the pace.
