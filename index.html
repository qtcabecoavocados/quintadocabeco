The Farm

An avocado grove between the hills and the sea

Tucked into the gentle folds of the countryside above Tavira, Quinta do Cabeço is a family farm where rows of avocado trees give way to open sky and the scent of citrus. Here, mornings begin with birdsong and the soft green of the grove, and evenings end under a wide, unhurried sunset.

The home has been kept simple and honest — natural materials, whitewashed walls, and terracotta underfoot — a place to breathe out and let the Algarve set the pace.

The House

Space to gather, corners to retreat

Light-filled rooms open onto shaded terraces and a garden of olive, fig, and lemon. Inside, a calm palette of stone and linen; outside, a pool to cool the afternoon and a long table made for lingering dinners.

Surroundings

Tavira, its river, and the islands beyond

One of the Algarve's most graceful towns, Tavira unfolds along a slow river crossed by a Roman bridge, its lanes lined with tiled façades and quiet squares. From here, a short ferry carries you to the white sands of Ilha de Tavira and the still lagoons of the Ria Formosa.

Salt pans and flamingos, market mornings, long lunches of grilled fish and local wine — the eastern Algarve rewards those who slow down.
