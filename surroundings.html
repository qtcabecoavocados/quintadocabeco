Surroundings

Tavira, its river,
and the islands beyond

One of the Algarve's most graceful towns, minutes from white-sand island beaches.

The town

A slow river and a Roman bridge

One of the Algarve's most graceful towns, Tavira unfolds along a slow river crossed by a Roman bridge, its lanes lined with tiled façades and quiet squares. Markets, churches, and riverside cafés reward an aimless morning's wander.

From here, a short ferry carries you to the white sands of Ilha de Tavira and the still lagoons of the Ria Formosa.

Beyond

Salt pans, flamingos, and long lunches

Salt pans and flamingos, market mornings, long lunches of grilled fish and local wine — the eastern Algarve rewards those who slow down. Cycle the coastal paths, swim from the island sandbanks, or simply return to the grove as the light softens.
