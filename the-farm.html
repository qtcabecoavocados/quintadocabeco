The Farm

An avocado grove between
the hills and the sea

A working family farm above Tavira, where the light is warm and the days move slowly.

The grove

Rows of avocado, olive, and citrus

Tucked into the gentle folds of the countryside above Tavira, Quinta do Cabeço is a family farm where rows of avocado trees give way to open sky and the scent of citrus. Mornings begin with birdsong and the soft green of the grove; evenings end under a wide, unhurried sunset.

The land is worked with care and in season you are welcome to wander the trees and pick what is ripe — avocados, lemons, and figs among them.

A slower pace

Room to breathe out

The home has been kept simple and honest — natural materials, whitewashed walls, and terracotta underfoot — a place to let the Algarve set the pace. Beyond the garden, the grove stretches toward the hills, quiet but for the wind and the birds.
